### Notes and caveats

- Figures are drawn from public reporting (Crunchbase News, TechCrunch, Bloomberg, CNBC, company blogs) between May 2025 and May 2026. Some valuations changed within the window — where multiple rounds happened (Harvey, Sierra, Perplexity, Ramp, Anthropic), the most recent confirmed number is shown alongside earlier ones.
- "Bay Area" here means the SF Peninsula/South Bay outside SF proper. Anduril, while California, is in Orange County (Costa Mesa) — not Bay Area.
- Some entries (Stripe, Brex, Rippling, Hugging Face) raised via secondary/tender rather than a primary financing in the window; included because the transaction set a new mark-to-market valuation.
- A few rounds (Humans&, Unconventional AI, Distyl) are early-stage but headline-grabbing; revenue/traction is less validated than for later-stage names on the list.
