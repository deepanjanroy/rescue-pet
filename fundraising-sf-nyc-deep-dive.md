# Deeper Dive: SF Proper and NYC Proper Fundraising (May 2025 – May 2026)

A more detailed look at companies headquartered specifically in San Francisco proper or New York City proper. For each round: amount, valuation, lead investors, date, what the company does, and known traction where reported.

Caveats up front:
- Some companies have HQs that are debated/recently moved (Crusoe moved its HQ to SF in 2025; Decagon expanded a major office in SF; Eon has dual-presence with Tel Aviv). These are flagged inline.
- Where multiple rounds happened in the 12-month window, all are listed.
- Numbers are post-money valuations unless noted. Sources are listed at the end of the file.

## San Francisco (proper)

### Foundation models / core AI labs

**1. OpenAI** — Foundation models (ChatGPT, GPT, Sora, Codex). 
- **Mar 2025:** $40B primary round led by SoftBank at a ~$300B post-money valuation, with tranches.
- **Late 2025/early 2026 secondary/tender:** marked ~$500B.
- Reported $13B+ revenue run-rate by late 2025.

**2. Anthropic** — Foundation models (Claude family). 
- **Sep 2025:** $13B Series F at $183B post-money, led by ICONIQ with Lightspeed, Fidelity, GIC.
- **2026:** $30B Series G at $380B post-money, led by GIC and Coatue.
- Crossed $7B+ ARR by early 2026 (per reporting).

**3. xAI** — Foundation models (Grok). Note: HQ is Palo Alto, not SF — included only as a reference, not counted here.

**4. World Labs** — Spatial intelligence / 3D world models, founded by Fei-Fei Li. 
- **2025:** ~$1B raised across rounds; multi-billion valuation; backers include a16z, Radical Ventures, NEA.

**5. Standard Intelligence** — AI research lab. 
- **2025:** $75M Series A led by Spark Capital and Sequoia at a $425M pre-money valuation.

**6. Unconventional AI** — New AI lab from Naveen Rao (ex-Databricks AI head). 
- **Dec 2025:** $475M seed at $4.5B; investors include General Catalyst.

**7. Humans&** — "Human-centric" AI lab. 
- **2025:** $480M seed at $4.48B.

### What jumps out

- **SF is dominated by foundation models and AI-native applications.** All the largest single rounds in the window happened in SF (OpenAI, Anthropic, Scale, Databricks, Cursor). The center of gravity for capital is here.
- **NYC's standouts are fintech-adjacent.** Ramp ($32B) and Kalshi ($22B) are the two clear NYC headline names; both are AI-tinted but their core moats are payments, regulation, and distribution. Rogo similarly applies AI on top of the city's existing strength (investment banking).
- **Round cadence is unusually fast.** Harvey raised four times in 14 months ($3B → $5B → $8B → $11B). Sierra, Decagon, Cursor, Ramp, Kalshi, and Mercor each more than doubled valuation inside the window.
- **"Headline" valuations are increasingly disconnected from primary capital deployed.** Mark-to-market via tender offers (Stripe, Brex, Rippling) now sets reference prices for very large companies that aren't actually deploying fresh primary capital.
